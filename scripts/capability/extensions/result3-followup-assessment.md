Assessment date: 2026-10-07. This is an evidence and feasibility review, not a new experiment or a change to frozen manuscript conclusions. The scientific question is which molecular phenotypes retain likelihood-based mutation-effect information, and whether ranking and quantitative prediction fail at different points.

The subsequently authorized [multi-endpoint program](phenotype-program.md) broadens acquisition and implementation beyond this initial local-data assessment. The recommendations below are the historical assessment, not a restriction to one additional phenotype or a veto on new public datasets.

## Stability: distinguish available summaries from usable prediction inputs

The frozen stability comparison uses 25,856 singles at 5,664 sites in 101 backgrounds/families. `results/R4/gate_stability_20260924/panel.json` retains all-model Spearman summaries for two control sets. The MSE-qualified S = ident + geom + chem + G yields thirteen historical all-split pointwise positives, but `primary_likelihood_spearman.licensed=false` explicitly makes that ranking result a sensitivity. The correlation-qualified S2 additionally includes composition and mutation-local profile (`comp`, `prof2`); `secondary_likelihood_spearman.licensed=true` has only one all-split pointwise positive, ProGen3-3B. Both evaluate MSE-trained predictions, and neither has the requested panel-simultaneous ranking inference. Thus the existing record already warns against concluding that stability ranking generalizes broadly from the thirteen-count sensitivity alone. The older 412-background megascale panel is a different support and raw-score comparison, not this matched nested analysis.

`manuscript/data/prediction-details/stability-samples.csv.gz` supplies exact sample/variant identities, combined ddG and complete trypsin/chymotrypsin delta labels. `results/R4/stability_likelihood_replay_20260927/` retains group MSE vectors and outer-fold records, not original per-variant prediction ordering or serialized inner memberships. Group MSE cannot reconstruct Spearman.

Current local recovery under `results/shared/prediction_details_replay_20261005/L20reproduction/` is more advanced than old handoff prose but still incomplete:

- Baseline-only stability recovery has completed receipts for all 33 models, including the separate RITA continuation.
- Native scalar archives and paired stability predictions exist for five models: the three ByGPT5 sizes, DialoGPT-small and Galactica-1.3B. None is ProGen3-3B or ProLLaMA Stage 2.
- These five paired products are labelled `reproduced_metric_mismatch`, not accepted recovered originals. Baseline group losses agree near machine precision; augmented group-loss differences reach approximately 10⁻⁶. Original extraction identity/code equivalence remains unresolved. They can support explicitly local exploratory work, not an unqualified full-panel claim.
- Likelihood/paired recovery is paused. Original compact scalar archives or original held-out predictions should be recovered before authorizing more scoring. ProteinGym rank features cannot fill missing stability states.

### Preparation that can be completed without H200

1. Freeze one ordered sample registry using the current cohort hash, WT/state identities, substitution indices, labels and family groups. Keep all three endpoint labels on the same support; validate the absolute-state and WT channel joins from the original parquet source as well as exported deltas.
2. Create a per-model recovery manifest that distinguishes original scalar archives, original OOF, baseline-only replay, paired local replay with discrepancies, and unavailable inputs. For scoring, retain only required WT/mutant states, exact native interface/checkpoint identity, source hashes and expected sample order. Do not request token-level arrays: scalar likelihood is sufficient here.
3. First recover/reaggregate within-background Spearman from the existing MSE-trained control/control+M predictions for both S and S2. S gives the same-prediction ranking-versus-MSE comparison; S2 is the historically correlation-qualified ranking comparison. Keep them separate and designate their inference families before analysis. Average split contrasts within biological family and use 10,000 joint family draws with simultaneous inference across the declared 33-model comparison. Preserve ties and explicitly report nonestimable backgrounds; do not pool variants across backgrounds or discard groups based on results.
4. Optionally prepare nested fits to standardized within-background rank targets as a separately named readout-objective sensitivity. Preserve both historically declared S and S2 and their metric-specific qualification; do not silently replace the correlation-qualified S2 by the weaker MSE-qualified S. The prior profile failure was quantitative-calibration failure, not failure to predict ranking. Preserve recorded outer partitions; any reconstructed inner partitions must be labelled as regenerated from the historical recipe, not verified original serialized memberships.
5. For channel robustness, refit each channel and the combined reference on identical variants and splits, with channel-specific training-only calibration. The nonlinear response block requires channel absolute/WT measurements, not delta labels alone. Alternatively, evaluate combined-trained OOF against each channel, but label that as metric reevaluation rather than channel-specific training.
6. Declare channel increments and their paired difference before fitting; use shared family draws and a stated model/channel multiplicity family. The two frozen positives can receive focused descriptive attention, but were selected on related combined measurements and are not independent confirmatory tests. A nonsignificant channel difference does not establish equivalent effects.

The same scalar scores serve combined, ranking and both channel endpoints. If originals remain unavailable after remote inspection, H200 need only score genuinely missing model–state combinations; do not automatically rerun all 33 models. No such inference was started for this review.

## Cross-phenotype consistency

The ProteinGym and abundance-ranking positive sets are identical: fourteen models under their respective three-split criteria. Both stability-MSE positives, ProGen3-3B and ProLLaMA Stage 2, belong to that set. Neither passes the abundance-MSE criterion; its two positives are ProGen2-xlarge and ProteinGLM. This is useful descriptive consistency, not proof that effect sizes covary or that one latent biological capability explains the panels.

Local source tables are `manuscript/data/local-increments-source.csv`, `external-abundance-source.csv` and `stability-panel-source.csv`; panel receipts provide their support and inference definitions. Compare split-mean effect estimates rather than p-values. Plot rank–rank separately from rank–MSE, retain units and baseline differences, and mark unresolved effects rather than treating them as zeros.

Use the existing sixteen release groups for equal-checkpoint-within-release summaries and leave-one-release-out checks. Releases themselves are not sixteen independent training histories; Llama2/ProLLaMA ancestry deserves a merged-parent or joint-exclusion sensitivity. ProteinGym contains stability assays, so assess shared proteins/families before any joint uncertainty calculation. A non-stability/overlap-excluded ProteinGym sensitivity is more interpretable than correlating all three unqualified panels.

Current local ProteinGym reconstruction in `results/extensions/information_progression_20261006/full/` supplies exact OOF/group contrasts for a separately labelled uncertainty analysis; it is not missing original H200 evidence. Abundance/stability group-vector coverage still needs explicit recovery checks. Descriptive model-level summaries require neither those missing vectors nor H200.

## Third-phenotype options and cleaner alternatives

`data/proteingym_raw/DMS_substitutions.csv` and all 217 processed assay tables are local. Category metadata and exact headline membership give:

| Endpoint category | Local assays / protein IDs | Already analyzed anchor assays / groups / sampled singles | Assessment |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Activity | 43 / 40 | 38 / 34 / 4,249 | Best functional sensitivity, but fluorescence, uptake, complementation and growth surrogates require assay-level adjudication; not all are direct catalytic activity. |
| Binding | 13 / 12 | 12 / 11 / 911 | Potential interface-related sensitivity with few families and heterogeneous direct/reporter measurements; not independent confirmation. |
| Expression | 18 / 18 | 16 / 16 / 1,935 | Includes abundance, trafficking and insertion; likely redundant with abundance unless a clearly distinct endpoint is isolated. |
| Organismal fitness | 77 / 64 | 69 / 53 / 7,966 | Large but mechanistically heterogeneous and already central to the mixed anchor; weak third-phenotype choice. |
| Clinical pathogenicity | No independent clinical-outcome cohort identified | Not established | Not recommended as the next molecular phenotype; evolutionary confounding and distance from direct measurement remain substantial. |

Groups may span endpoint categories and must not be summed as independent units. Metadata category membership is not endpoint qualification. Processed ProteinGym tables do not generally retain replicate/count information; only eleven selected raw assay tables are staged locally. Domainome's `normalized_fitness` is an abundance-PCA readout, not an unused independent activity channel; its raw-score ZIP is not currently staged. Previously support-audited external candidates also lack adequate released labels or independent family coverage, so no ready independent third cohort was established.

Two alternatives are preferable now:

- **Fixed-prediction endpoint stratification:** evaluate existing held-out predictions within manually qualified activity, binding, abundance/expression and fitness categories, retaining family weighting and reporting coverage. This can reveal where current mutation-effect gains weaken without new inference. It is retrospective within-anchor sensitivity, not external replication. Refit-on-stratum analysis, if wanted, must be separately named.
- **Same-variant activity–abundance labels:** six local protein pairs have substantial exact single-state label intersections, but only 1–4 rows per pair were sampled in both original assays. Prefer attaching the second label to one predeclared already-scored support rather than demanding intersection of two independently sampled score tables. Within-assay score ranks from differently sampled panels must not be merged as if they were native scores. Six protein pairs are below the historical eight-group floor even before grouping; use a focused descriptive study, not a generalization headline. Conditioning on measured abundance is explicitly label-assisted and does not establish abundance-independent function from sequence alone.

If an independent activity/binding cohort is pursued later, first freeze accession/WT and exact mutation mapping, direct endpoint definition/sign/units, single-substitution and censoring filters, family/near-duplicate grouping, anchor and homology overlap exclusions, replicate reliability, phenotype-specific baseline qualification, metrics and family-level uncertainty. Join every model's existing state coverage before producing a minimal missing-state inference manifest. New protein/family coverage—not merely more mutations on already analyzed proteins—must justify the cost. None of these qualification gates should be relaxed to fill a third-phenotype slot.
